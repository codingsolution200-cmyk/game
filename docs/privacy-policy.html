2. Information We Do Not Collect

SapSidi does not directly collect or store personally identifiable information such as your name, email address, phone number, or precise location. The app does not require account registration.

3. Third-Party Services

The app uses third-party services that may process limited technical or usage-related data:

7. Data Retention and Deletion
